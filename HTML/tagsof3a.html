Understanding Ethical Hacking and Penetraion Testing

An ethical hacker's goal is to analyze the security posture of a network's or system's infrastructure in an effort to identify and possibly exploit any security weaknesses found and then determine if a compromise is possible.
This process is called security penetration testing or ethical hacking

Why Do We Need to Do Penetration Testing?

Well,first of all,as someone who is responsible for securing and defending a network/system,you want to find any possible paths of compromise before the bad guys do

Threat Actors:

This are 4 types:
1.Organized crime:
organised crime goes where the money is.

2.Hacktivist:
This type of threat actor is not motivated by money.
Hacktivists are looking to make a point or to further their beliefs, using cybercrime as their method of attack.

3.State-Sponsored Attackers:
Cyber war and cyber espionage are two terms that fit into this category.

4.Insider threat:
An insider threat is a threat that comes from inside an organization.

OWASP WSTG:

OWASP WSTG is a step-by-step guide for testing the security of websites and web applications. It tells security testers

  • What to test
  • How to test
  • Which attacks to try
  • How to fix the problems

What kind of attacks OWASP covers

    CSRF
    XXE
